# Carom

A little spin. A different game. Carom puts two paddles on a dark court, drops a pair of amber obstacles between them, and lets the angles do the talking. Take on the machine in Solo or bring a friend for a local Versus duel.

The opening serve gives you a moment to settle in. Find the ball, meet it with your paddle, and send it back. Each paddle hit adds speed, turning a measured exchange into a quick contest of positioning and nerve.

Move as you make contact and the shot starts to curve. Slide down to add clockwise spin, or up to turn the other way. The luminous trail shows the bend as it fades. Catch the edge of an obstacle and a direct attack becomes a bank shot that asks your opponent to read the court again.

The first player to eleven needs a two-point lead to finish it. At ten-all, every return buys another chance: hold the center, chase the angle, or risk a moving hit that sends the next rally somewhere new.
